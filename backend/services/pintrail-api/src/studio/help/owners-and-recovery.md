This page is for **admins**.

## Owners

Every artifact has an **owner**: the person who added it. The owner is the only editor who can change or delete it. Admins can change anything.

Some artifacts have **no owner**, because they were added before the Studio recorded who added what, or by a script (the *Example:* artifacts are like this). Only an admin can change them.

## Giving an artifact to someone else

1. Open the artifact and click **Edit**.
2. Under **Owner** (only admins see it), choose the author.
3. Click **Save changes**.

The new owner can now change it. The [history](/studio/help/history) records the change of owner.

> **Example:** A student starts *Old Chapel* and then drops the course. You give it to the student taking over that building, and they carry on from where it was.

The review list's **No owner** section lists artifacts that need an owner. To move all of someone's work at once, delete or [manage their account](/studio/help/managing-authors#deleting-an-author) and choose who gets it.

## Restoring a deleted artifact

Click **Recently deleted** in the sidebar. It lists deleted artifacts, newest first, with who deleted them and when.

Click **Restore** next to one. It comes back **with everything that was deleted along with it**: what was nested inside, its links, photos, and tags. Its history is kept, with the delete and the restore recorded.

> **Note:** If an artifact was deleted because its parent was, restore the parent: that brings back both.
