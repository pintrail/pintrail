## Signing in

**I forgot my password.** Ask your instructor to reset it. They'll give you a temporary one, and you'll choose your own when you sign in.

**It keeps asking me to change my password.** You need to choose a new password before you can do anything else. Type the temporary one as your current password, then a new one of at least 12 characters, twice.

**It sent me to my profile.** Fill in your full name and save. The Studio needs it before you start, so it can show who added what.

## Adding and editing

**"A top-level artifact needs a location."** Click the map where it is, or choose the artifact it sits inside under **Inside (parent)**.

**There's no Edit or Delete button.** It isn't yours. You can still add an artifact inside it. Ask its owner or your instructor to change it. See [Who can do what](/studio/help/who-can-do-what).

**Where do I add the description, kind, and tags?** On the artifact's page, after you create it: click **Edit**. The New artifact form only asks for the name and place. See [Adding an artifact](/studio/help/adding-an-artifact).

**Where are links and photos?** On the artifact's page, in the **Links** and **Media** cards, not on the Edit form.

**My approved artifact went back to "Ready for review".** That happens whenever it changes after approval, so the change gets checked. See [Review, and what the app shows](/studio/help/review-and-the-app#changing-an-approved-artifact).

**I can't link someone else's artifact to my topic.** Linking changes the artifact, so only its owner or an admin can. See [Linking artifacts to a topic](/studio/help/linking-topics#who-can-link).

**I deleted something by mistake.** Ask your instructor: an admin can restore it, with everything that was inside it. See [Deleting and restoring](/studio/help/deleting).

## Location and the map

**The pin is in the wrong place.** Edit the artifact, zoom in, and drag the pin (or click the right spot). A nested artifact that uses its parent's location moves when the parent does.

**"Use my location" doesn't work.** Allow location access when your phone asks. If you said no before, turn it back on in your browser's settings. Indoors, the reading can be far off; step outside or tap the map instead. See [Using the Studio on your phone](/studio/help/phone).

**My artifact isn't on the map.** It has no location of its own and no parent with one. It's listed under **No location** below the map. Give it a pin.

## Photos

**A photo shows "failed".** Remove it with the **✕** and try again. If it keeps failing, try a JPEG instead of a HEIC, or a smaller file. See [Photos and PDFs](/studio/help/media).

**I can't upload a video or audio.** Not supported yet.

## The app

**My artifact isn't in the app.** Only approved artifacts are, and only if everything they're inside is approved too. Check its status, and its parent's.

## Still stuck?

Tell your instructor what you were doing, the name of the artifact, and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
