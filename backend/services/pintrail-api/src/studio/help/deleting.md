**Delete** is at the top of an artifact's page. It asks you to confirm first.

> **Watch out:** Deleting an artifact **also deletes everything nested inside it**: the roof, the rooms, the systems, with their links and photos. To keep something nested, first **Edit** it and move it to a different parent.

## Who can delete

- **Editors** can delete an artifact only if it **and everything inside it** are theirs, and **none of it is approved**. That way one mistaken click can't take a classmate's work, or reviewed work, with it.
- **Admins** can delete anything.

If there's no **Delete** button, one of those rules applies. Ask your instructor.

## Deleting a topic

Deleting a [topic](/studio/help/topics) removes the topic. The artifacts linked to it stay, without the link.

## Deleting a trail

A trail has its own **Delete** button. It deletes the trail only; the artifacts it stopped at aren't affected.

## Getting something back

Deleted artifacts aren't gone for good. An admin can restore one, with **everything that was deleted with it**, and its links and photos, from **Recently deleted**. Ask your instructor. See [Owners and recovering deleted work](/studio/help/owners-and-recovery#restoring-a-deleted-artifact).

Its [history](/studio/help/history) is kept either way.
