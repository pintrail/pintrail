Click **Map** at the top of the sidebar to see every artifact on one map.

## Reading the map

- Each **marker** is an artifact with its own location.
- Artifacts **nested** inside it, using its location, are listed in its popup rather than stacked on top of it. Click a marker to see them.
- The **fill colour** shows the [kind](/studio/help/kinds). The **ring** shows the review status: grey for draft, orange for ready for review, green for approved.
- Click a marker to see its name, kind, status, and tags, and the artifacts inside it. Click a name to open that artifact.

## Filtering

Use **Kind**, **Status**, and **Tag** above the map to show only some artifacts. The count above the map updates as you filter.

> **Example:** Choose **Tag: solar** to see every solar artifact on campus. Choose **Status: Draft** to see what still needs work. Choose **Kind: building** to see which buildings have been added.

## What it's good for

- **Spotting a pin in the wrong place**, like a rooftop pinned to the building next door.
- **Finding gaps**: parts of campus nobody has covered yet.
- **Planning a trail**: seeing which artifacts are close together.

## Artifacts with no location

Below the map, **No location** lists any artifact that has no location of its own and no parent with one. They can't appear in the app until they get one. Open each and give it a pin, or put it inside a building.

[Topics](/studio/help/topics) aren't on this map, since they aren't places. A topic's own page shows its linked artifacts on a map.
