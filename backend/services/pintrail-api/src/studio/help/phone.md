The Studio works in your phone's web browser, so you can add an artifact while standing in front of it: its name, your location, and a photo, then fill in the rest later at a desk.

## Getting around

- The **artifact list** is behind the **menu button** (☰) at the top left. Tap an artifact to open it, and the list closes.
- **Help** is the **?** at the top of the screen.
- To **scroll the page**, drag outside the map. Dragging on the map moves the map.

## Putting the pin where you are

On the New artifact or Edit form, tap **Use my location**, under the map.

1. The first time, your phone asks whether to share your location with the site. **Allow** it.
2. The pin goes where your phone thinks you are, with a shaded circle showing how sure it is.
3. **Check the pin**, and drag it to the exact spot if needed.

Outdoors, your location is usually right to within 5 to 15 metres. Indoors it can be much worse. Step outside, or place the pin by tapping the map.

> **Watch out:** If your phone says location access is blocked, turn it back on for the site in your browser's settings (on an iPhone: **Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Safari Websites**), or tap the map to place the pin.

## Taking photos

On an artifact's page, the **Media** card has **Take photo**. It opens your camera, and the photo uploads as soon as you take it. **+ Upload** picks photos from your library instead. See [Photos and PDFs](/studio/help/media).

## A quick on-site routine

> **Example:** At the bike rack you want to add: tap ☰, then **+ New**. Type *Bike rack by the library*, tap **Use my location**, check the pin, and tap **Create**. On its page, tap **Take photo** and take two pictures. Later, at a laptop, click **Edit** to add the kind, description, and tags, and add a source link.
