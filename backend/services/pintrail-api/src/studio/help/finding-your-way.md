## The parts of the screen

- **The top bar** has **Help** (this manual), your **name and photo** (your profile and password), and **Sign out**. Admins also see **Admin panel**.
- **The sidebar on the left** lists every artifact. Artifacts that sit inside another one are indented under it, so a building's roof and rooms appear just below the building. Click an artifact to open it.
- **The main area** shows whatever you opened: an artifact, a form, the map, a trail, or a page of this manual.

## The sidebar buttons

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **+ New** | Adds an artifact. See [Adding an artifact](/studio/help/adding-an-artifact). |
| **Map** | Shows every artifact on one map. See [The map](/studio/help/map). |
| **Trails** | Lists the trails. See [Trails](/studio/help/trails). |
| **Topics** | Lists the topics. See [Topics](/studio/help/topics). |
| **Only mine** | Shows only the artifacts you added. |

Admins also see **Review** (artifacts waiting to be approved, with a count) and **Recently deleted**.

## What the little symbols mean

- **The coloured dot** before each artifact in the list is its review status: grey for *draft*, orange for *ready for review*, green for *approved*. See [Review, and what the app shows](/studio/help/review-and-the-app).
- **The small grey label** (like `BUILDING` or `INSTALLATION`) is the artifact's [kind](/studio/help/kinds). A purple `TOPIC` label marks a [topic](/studio/help/topics).
- **The six dots** (⋮⋮) next to a link or a trail stop are a grip: drag it to change the order.

## Getting help

- **Hover** over a button, label, or symbol for a short tip. With a keyboard, tab to it.
- Click a **?** beside a heading or a field to open the right page of this manual in a panel on the right. Close it with the **×** or the <kbd>Esc</kbd> key.
- Press <kbd>?</kbd> anywhere (except while typing in a box) to open the whole manual.

## Links you can share

The address in your browser changes as you move around, so you can bookmark an artifact or send its address to a classmate. They'll need a Studio account to open it.

> **Tip:** On a phone, the artifact list is behind the menu button (☰) at the top left. See [Using the Studio on your phone](/studio/help/phone).
