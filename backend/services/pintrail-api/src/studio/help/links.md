Every fact in a description should come from somewhere a reader can check. Add those pages in the **Links** card on the artifact's page, below the location.

## Adding a link

1. Paste the address into the first box. You can leave off `https://`.
2. Optionally, say what it is in the second box: *Source for the energy figures*.
3. Click **+ Add link**.

Pintrail reads the page and shows a preview, with its title, summary, and picture, like a link pasted into a chat app. This takes a few seconds. Some sites don't offer a preview; the link still works and shows its address instead.

> **Example:** `umass.edu/facilities/utilities`, with the note *Source: what the plant supplies to campus*.

## Changing links

- **Edit**: click the pencil to change the address or the note. A new address gets a fresh preview.
- **Reorder**: drag a link by its grip (the six dots on its left). Or click the grip and use the <kbd>↑</kbd> and <kbd>↓</kbd> keys. Put the most important source first.
- **Remove**: click the **×**.

## Good sources

- **Official pages** first: UMass Facilities, the sustainability office, the project's own page, a news release.
- **Specific pages**, not home pages: the page that actually has the number you used.
- **Worth reading next**: a good article or video is fine to add too, after your sources. Say what it is in the note.

> **Note:** Links, like photos and topics, count as part of the artifact. Changing one on an approved artifact sends it back for review.
