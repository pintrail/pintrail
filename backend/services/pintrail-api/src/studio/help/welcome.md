The **Pintrail Studio** is where the campus content for the Pintrail app gets written. People walking around campus with the Pintrail app on their phone get a nudge when they're near something worth knowing about, and can read about it, see photos, and follow walks. Everything they read starts here.

You use the Studio in a web browser, on a laptop or on your phone: **https://pintrail.cs.umass.edu/studio**.

## What you make in the Studio

- **Artifacts** are the things worth stopping for: a building, a green roof, a battery system, a mural, a rain garden. Each has a name, a place on the map, a description, sources, and photos. [More about artifacts](/studio/help/artifacts).
- **Topics** are shared pages that many artifacts link to, like *LEED certification* or *Valley Bike Share*. You write the shared part once. [More about topics](/studio/help/topics).
- **Trails** are walks through artifacts in a set order, with a note at each stop, like a *Campus energy walk*. [More about trails](/studio/help/trails).

> **Example:** The *Integrative Learning Center* is an artifact (a building). Its *green roof* is another artifact, inside it. The building links to the topic *LEED certification*, with the note *Gold, 2013*. A trail called *Where the rain goes* might stop at the green roof, then at a rain garden across campus.

## Your first five steps

1. **Sign in** and choose your own password, then fill in your profile. See [Signing in and your profile](/studio/help/signing-in).
2. **Look at the examples.** Artifacts whose names start with *Example:* show what a finished artifact looks like. Open one from the list on the left.
3. **Add your first artifact.** Click **+ New**, give it a name, and put a pin on the map. See [Adding an artifact](/studio/help/adding-an-artifact).
4. **Fill it in**: a description, a kind, tags, a source link, and a photo. The artifact's page shows a checklist.
5. **Submit it for review.** Your instructor checks it and approves it, or sends it back with a note. Only approved artifacts appear in the app. See [Submitting for review](/studio/help/submitting-for-review).

## Getting help while you work

- Press <kbd>?</kbd> anywhere in the Studio (when you're not typing in a box) to open this manual, or click **Help** at the top of the page.
- Hover over a button or a label to see a short tip about it.
- Click a small **?** next to a heading or a field to open the part of the manual about it, in a panel on the right. Your form stays as it was underneath, so you don't lose what you typed.
