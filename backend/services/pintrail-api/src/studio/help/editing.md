Open the artifact and click **Edit** at the top of its page. You can change the **name**, **kind**, **parent**, **description**, **tags**, and **location**. Click **Save changes**, or **Cancel** to leave it as it was.

Links, photos, and topics aren't on the Edit form: change them right on the artifact's page, in their own cards.

## Who can edit

You can edit the artifacts **you** added. Admins can edit any artifact. If there's no **Edit** button, it isn't yours. See [Who can do what](/studio/help/who-can-do-what).

## Editing an approved artifact

When you change an approved artifact, including adding or removing a link, photo, or topic, it goes back to **Ready for review**, and leaves the app until it's approved again. That way what's in the app is always what was checked.

> **Tip:** Collect your changes and make them in one go, rather than one small edit a day, so it only needs reviewing once.

## Moving it

Change **Inside (parent)** to move the artifact into a different parent, or to **none (top level)**. Everything nested in it moves too. A top-level artifact needs its own location.

## If saving fails

The form stays open with a red note at the top saying what to fix, and keeps what you typed.

| Message | Fix |
|---|---|
| *A top-level artifact needs a location* | Click the map, or choose a parent. |
| *Enter both latitude and longitude* | Fill in both boxes, or clear both. |
| *That parent is inside this artifact already* | You can't put something inside its own child. Choose another parent. |

## Every change is kept

Nothing is lost by editing. The [history](/studio/help/history) at the bottom of the page records each change: what it was, what it became, who changed it, and when.
