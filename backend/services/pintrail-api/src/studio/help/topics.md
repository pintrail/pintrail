A **topic** is one shared page that many artifacts link to. You write what they have in common **once**, and every linked artifact shows it.

> **Example:** Six bike share stations around campus all belong to *Valley Bike Share*. Instead of writing *"Valley Bike Share is the Pioneer Valley's public bike share..."* on six artifacts, you write it on the topic and link the six stations to it. Fix a mistake in the topic, and it's fixed on all six.

> **Example:** Several buildings are LEED certified. Each building's own page says what's special about it; the *LEED certification* topic explains what LEED is. Each link carries a short note, like *Gold, 2013*.

## What a topic has

A topic is like an artifact without a place. It has a **name**, a **description**, **tags**, **source links**, **photos**, a **review status**, and a **history**. It has **no** location, parent, or kind.

Because it has no place:

- it isn't a pin on the [map](/studio/help/map) by itself, though its page shows every linked artifact on a map at once;
- it can't be a [trail](/studio/help/trails) stop;
- nothing can be nested inside it.

## Where topics show up

- On each **linked artifact's page**, a **Topics** card shows the topic's name, the note on the link, and the topic's description.
- On the **topic's own page**, **Linked artifacts** lists every artifact that links to it, on a map.
- Under **Topics** in the sidebar, every topic is listed with how many artifacts link to it.
- In the Pintrail app, an approved topic appears with the artifacts that link to it.

## When to use a topic

Use a topic when **several separate places share a story** that's worth explaining properly.

| You have | Use |
|---|---|
| Several bike share stations, one system | A topic, *Valley Bike Share* |
| Several LEED buildings | A topic, *LEED certification*, with the level as the note on each link |
| A green roof that's part of one building | [Nesting](/studio/help/nesting): put it inside the building |
| A theme people search by, nothing to explain | A [tag](/studio/help/tags), like *solar* |
| A walk past several places in order | A [trail](/studio/help/trails) |

See [Kinds, tags, topics, or nesting?](/studio/help/tags-kinds-topics) for a side-by-side comparison.

## Notes on links

Each link between an artifact and a topic can carry a short **note** (up to 200 characters) for what's particular to that artifact.

> **Example:** On the link from *Integrative Learning Center* to *LEED certification*: *Gold, LEED NC v2009*. On a bike share station's link: *15 docks*.
