A [topic](/studio/help/topics) is one shared page that many artifacts link to. Here's how to start one.

## Before you start

Check the **Topics** list in the sidebar first: the topic you want may already exist. Two topics for the same thing defeats the purpose.

## Creating it

1. Click **Topics** at the top of the sidebar, then **+ New topic**.
2. **Name** it after the shared idea, not one artifact: *LEED certification*, not *Integrative Learning Center LEED*.
3. Write a **description** of what's true of **every** artifact that will link to it.
4. Add **tags** if they help.
5. Click **Create topic**.

The topic opens. Like an artifact, it starts as a **Draft**, and has **Links** and **Media** cards for sources and pictures. Then link the artifacts it's about: see [Linking artifacts to a topic](/studio/help/linking-topics).

## What to write

Write the part that **doesn't change** from one artifact to the next. Put what does change on each artifact, or in the short note on each link.

> **Example:** *LEED certification*
>
> Topic description: what LEED is, who awards it, and what the levels (Certified, Silver, Gold, Platinum) mean.
>
> On each link: the building's level and year, like *Gold, 2013*.
>
> On each building's own page: what that building does that earned it.

> **Example:** *Valley Bike Share*
>
> Topic description: what the bike share is, how to unlock a bike, what it costs, and why it matters.
>
> Each station's page: where it is and anything particular about it. On its link: *15 docks*.

## Good topic names

| Good | Not so good |
|---|---|
| *LEED certification* | *LEED buildings* (the topic is the idea; the buildings link to it) |
| *Valley Bike Share* | *Bike share stations* |
| *Combined heat and power* | *CHP* (spell it out; add *CHP* as a tag) |

## Review

Topics go through [review](/studio/help/review-and-the-app) like artifacts. The app shows a topic, and its links, only once it's approved.
