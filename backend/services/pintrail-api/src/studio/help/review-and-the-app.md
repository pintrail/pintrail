Nothing you write in the Studio reaches the Pintrail app until someone has checked it. Every artifact and topic has a **review status**:

| Status | Dot | What it means | In the app? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Draft** | grey | You're still working on it. | No |
| **Ready for review** | orange | You've submitted it; it's waiting for an admin. | No |
| **Approved** | green | An admin checked it and approved it. | Yes |

The dot appears before each artifact in the list on the left, and the status at the top of each artifact's page.

## How an artifact moves through review

1. Every new artifact starts as a **Draft**.
2. When it's finished, you click **Submit for review**. It becomes **Ready for review**, and your instructor sees it in their review list.
3. The admin either **approves** it, or **sends it back** with a note saying what to change. Sent back, it's a Draft again, with the note shown at the top of its Review card.
4. You fix it and submit it again.

Changed your mind after submitting? **Withdraw** takes it back to Draft.

See [Submitting for review](/studio/help/submitting-for-review) for the details.

## Changing an approved artifact

If you edit an approved artifact (or add or remove a link, photo, or topic), it goes back to **Ready for review**, so the change gets checked too. Until it's approved again, it's **out of the app**. So make changes in one go, then let it be reviewed once.

> **Note:** Admins' own changes don't send an artifact back for review.

## Things inside an unapproved artifact

An artifact appears in the app only if it **and everything above it** are approved. An approved green roof inside a building that's still a draft stays out of the app until the building is approved.

> **Example:** You add *Old Chapel* (draft) and *Bell tower* inside it, and the bell tower gets approved first. Explorers won't see the bell tower until *Old Chapel* is approved too, since it would otherwise appear with no building to belong to.

## Topics and trails

- **Topics** go through the same review. The app shows a topic, and the links to it, only once the topic is approved.
- **Trails** don't have a review status; they have [who can see it](/studio/help/sharing-a-trail). Only an admin can make one public.
