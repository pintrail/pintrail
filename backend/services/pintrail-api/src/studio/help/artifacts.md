An **artifact** is anything on campus worth stopping for. It's the basic building block of Pintrail: the app tells someone when they're near an artifact, and shows them what it is.

> **Example:** These are all artifacts: the *Central Heating Plant* (a building), its *Battery energy storage system* (equipment inside it), the *Integrative Learning Center green roof*, a *mural in the Student Union*, a *bike share station on Haigis Mall*.

## What an artifact has

| Part | What it is | Where to learn more |
|---|---|---|
| **Name** | What people call it. | [Naming an artifact](#naming-an-artifact), below |
| **Place** | A pin on the map, or its parent's location. | [Location](/studio/help/location) |
| **Parent** | The artifact it sits in or on, if any. | [Artifacts inside artifacts](/studio/help/nesting) |
| **Kind** | What sort of thing it is: building, room, artwork, installation, rooftop, or other. | [Kinds](/studio/help/kinds) |
| **Description** | What it is, why it matters, and how it works. | [Writing a good description](/studio/help/writing-descriptions) |
| **Tags** | Themes people search by, like *solar*. | [Tags](/studio/help/tags) |
| **Topics** | Shared pages it links to, like *LEED certification*. | [Topics](/studio/help/topics) |
| **Links** | The web pages your facts come from. | [Source links](/studio/help/links) |
| **Media** | Photos and PDFs. | [Photos and PDFs](/studio/help/media) |
| **Status** | Draft, ready for review, or approved. | [Review, and what the app shows](/studio/help/review-and-the-app) |

## What makes a good artifact

A good artifact is **one specific thing a person can find**, with a story worth a minute of their time.

- **Specific, not general.** *Lot 49 solar canopy* is an artifact. *Solar power on campus* is not; that's a [topic](/studio/help/topics) or a [trail](/studio/help/trails).
- **Findable.** Someone should be able to stand in front of it, or at least outside it. If the public can't reach it (a locked mechanical room), say so in the description.
- **Worth knowing.** It says something about sustainability on campus that a visitor wouldn't guess just by looking.

## Naming an artifact

Use the name people on campus would recognise, as short as you can make it while still being clear.

| Good | Not so good | Why |
|---|---|---|
| *Old Chapel* | *The Old Chapel building at UMass* | Short; the kind already says it's a building. |
| *Lot 49 solar canopy* | *Solar panels* | Specific: there are many solar panels on campus. |
| *Green roof* (inside the Integrative Learning Center) | *ILC green roof on top of the ILC* | Inside its building, it doesn't need to repeat the building's name. |

> **Tip:** Names starting with *Example:* are reserved for the worked examples. Don't start yours that way.

## Artifacts in the app

Only **approved** artifacts appear in the Pintrail app. Everything else, drafts included, is visible only in the Studio. See [Review, and what the app shows](/studio/help/review-and-the-app).
