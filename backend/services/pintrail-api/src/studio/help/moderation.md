This page is for **admins**. Besides **Authors**, the admin panel (click **Admin panel** at the top of the Studio) has two more sections, about what **explorers** (people using the Pintrail app) add.

## Comments

Explorers can comment on artifacts in the app. **Comments** lists them, with the comment, who wrote it, which artifact it's on, its status, and when it was posted.

- Comments marked **flagged** need a decision.
- **Hide** removes a comment from the public thread. **Restore** brings it back.

Hide comments that are abusive, off-topic advertising, or personal information about someone. Disagreement and criticism are fine.

## Trails

Explorers can build their own trails in the app and make them public. **Trails** lists every **public** trail, with its owner and number of stops. **Take down** deletes a public trail permanently.

Private trails, and ones shared only by link, aren't listed: moderation covers what other people can find, not what someone keeps to themselves.

Trails made in the Studio are managed here in the Studio, under **Trails**. See [Trails](/studio/help/trails).
