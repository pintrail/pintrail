An artifact's **kind** says what sort of thing it is. It's one choice from a short list, shown as a small grey label next to the artifact's name and used to colour the markers on the [map](/studio/help/map).

## The kinds

| Kind | Use it for | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| `building` | A whole building. | *Old Chapel*, *Central Heating Plant* |
| `room` | A space inside a building. | *ILC lobby*, *Room S331 teaching lab* |
| `artwork` | A mural, sculpture, or other piece of art. | *Student Union mural* |
| `installation` | Equipment or a feature of a site. | *Solar canopy*, *battery system*, *rain garden*, *bike rack*, *water bottle filler* |
| `rooftop` | A roof and what's on it. | *Green roof*, *rooftop solar* |
| `other` | Anything that fits none of these. | *A memorial bench*, *a campus tree* |

## Choosing a kind

Pick the one a visitor would use to describe it. When two fit, choose the more specific one: a solar array on a roof is an `installation` if the story is the panels, and a `rooftop` if the story is the roof as a whole.

> **Example:** The *Battery energy storage system* inside the heating plant is an `installation`: it's equipment, not a building or a room.

## New artifacts start as `other`

When you create an artifact you only give it a name and a place. It starts as `other`, and you choose its kind when you fill it in with **Edit**. The artifact's checklist reminds you.

## When nothing fits

Choose `other` and add a [tag](/studio/help/tags) that says what it is, like *bench* or *tree*. The list of kinds will grow, or be replaced by themes, once we see what people are adding, and tags are how we'll find out.

## Kinds versus tags

A kind is **one** answer to *what is it?* Tags are **any number** of answers to *what is it about?* A solar canopy is an `installation` (kind) about *solar* and *energy* (tags). See [Kinds, tags, topics, or nesting?](/studio/help/tags-kinds-topics)
