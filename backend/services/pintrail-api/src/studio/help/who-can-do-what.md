Every Studio account has a **role**. Your instructor sets it.

| Role | Who has it | What they can do |
|---|---|---|
| **Viewer** | Visitors and guests | Look at everything. Can't change anything. |
| **Editor** | Students and other authors | Add artifacts, topics, and trails, and change or delete the ones they made. |
| **Admin** | Instructors | Everything an editor can, for anyone's work, plus approve artifacts, make trails public, and manage accounts. |

## You change what you made

As an editor, you can edit only the artifacts, topics, and trails **you** added. Everyone can still see everything.

This keeps one mistaken click from changing a classmate's work. Each artifact shows who added it, near the top of its page.

> **Example:** Sam added *Old Chapel*. You can open it and read it, but there's no **Edit** button for you. You can still add a new artifact *inside* it, like *Old Chapel bell tower*, and that one is yours.

## What you can do with someone else's artifact

- **Add an artifact inside it.** Any editor can. That's how a group builds out one building together.
- **Put it on your trail.** Any artifact can be a stop on any trail.
- **Read its history** and see who added and changed what.

To change someone else's artifact, ask them, or ask your instructor, who can change anything or [give it to you](/studio/help/owners-and-recovery#giving-an-artifact-to-someone-else).

## Deleting

An editor can delete an artifact only if everything nested inside it is theirs too and none of it is approved. See [Deleting and restoring](/studio/help/deleting).

## Approved work

When you change an artifact after it's approved, it goes back to **Ready for review** so the change gets checked, and it leaves the app until it's approved again. See [Review, and what the app shows](/studio/help/review-and-the-app).
