Photos make an artifact. Add them, and any PDFs, in the **Media** card on the artifact's page.

## Uploading

1. Click **+ Upload** and choose one or more files.
2. Each new file shows **processing…** for a moment while Pintrail makes a phone-friendly version and a thumbnail. The card updates by itself.
3. When it's ready, the thumbnail appears.

On a phone there's also **Take photo**, which opens the camera and uploads the picture straight away. See [Using the Studio on your phone](/studio/help/phone).

## What you can upload

| Type | Files |
|---|---|
| Photos | JPEG, PNG, WebP, and HEIC (iPhone photos, converted for you) |
| Documents | PDF (the first page becomes the thumbnail) |

Up to 100 MB per file. Audio and video aren't supported yet.

## Good photos

- **Take them yourself**, or use ones you have permission to use. Don't copy photos from websites.
- **Show the thing itself**, clearly. One good close photo beats several distant ones.
- **Show it as a visitor would see it**, so they can recognise it when they get there.
- **No people's faces** without their permission.

> **Example:** For a green roof: one photo from where a visitor can see it, and one close up of the plants. For a battery system: the outside of its enclosure, since visitors can't go in.

## If something goes wrong

- **Shows failed**: remove it with the **✕** and upload it again. If it keeps failing, try a different format (a JPEG instead of a HEIC), or tell your instructor.
- **Stuck on processing** for more than a minute or two: reload the page. If it's still stuck, tell your instructor.

## Removing a photo

Click the **✕** under it, and confirm.

> **Note:** Adding or removing a photo on an approved artifact sends it back for review.
