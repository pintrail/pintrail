A [trail](/studio/help/trails) is a walk through artifacts in order. Here's how to start one.

## Creating it

1. Click **Trails** at the top of the sidebar, then **+ New trail**.
2. Give it a **title**: short and inviting.
3. Write a **description**: what the walk is about and why it's worth doing. Mention roughly how long it takes and where it starts.
4. Choose **who can see it**. Keep it **Private** while you build it. See [Sharing a trail](/studio/help/sharing-a-trail).
5. Click **Create trail**.

The trail opens. Now add its [stops](/studio/help/trail-stops).

> **Example:** Title: *Where the rain goes*. Description: *Follow rainwater across campus, from a planted roof to Campus Pond. About 30 minutes, starting at the Integrative Learning Center.*

## Good titles

| Good | Not so good |
|---|---|
| *Renewable energy on campus* | *Trail 1* |
| *Where the rain goes* | *Stormwater infrastructure tour of selected UMass sites* |

## Planning the walk

- **Pick a theme**, and five to ten stops that tell its story.
- **Walk it yourself** if you can, and check the order makes sense on the ground.
- **Think about the end**: finish somewhere people would want to be, or near where the next thing is.
- **Get the stops approved.** Explorers can only visit stops whose artifacts are approved.

## Changing a trail

Open it and click **Edit** to change the title, description, or who can see it. You can change only the trails you made; admins can change any.

**Delete** removes the trail. Its artifacts aren't affected.
