When an artifact is finished, submit it for review. Your instructor checks it, then approves it, which puts it in the app, or sends it back with a note.

## Before you submit

Check it has:

- a **name** people would recognise;
- a **pin in the right place** (zoom in and look);
- the right **kind**, and a few **tags**;
- a **description** that says what it is, why it matters, and how it works, with a real number where you have one;
- at least one **source link** for its facts;
- a **photo** you took.

The checklist at the top of a draft's page shows what's still missing. See [Writing a good description](/studio/help/writing-descriptions).

## Submitting

Open the artifact. In the **Review** card, click **Submit for review**. Its status changes to **Ready for review**, and it shows up in your instructor's review list.

**Changed your mind?** Click **Withdraw** to take it back to Draft and keep working.

## What comes back

- **Approved**: it's done, and it appears in the app (as long as whatever it's inside is approved too).
- **Sent back for changes**: it's a Draft again, and the **Review** card shows the reviewer's note at the top, saying what to change. Fix it, then click **Submit for review** again.

> **Example:** Sent back with *"The 40% figure needs a source. Add it as a link."* You add the link, and submit again.

The note also stays in the artifact's [history](/studio/help/history).

## After it's approved

If you change an approved artifact, it goes back to **Ready for review** and leaves the app until it's approved again. See [Review, and what the app shows](/studio/help/review-and-the-app#changing-an-approved-artifact).

## Topics

[Topics](/studio/help/topics) are submitted the same way, from the **Review** card on the topic's page.
