This page is for **admins**. Authors are the people who can sign in to the Studio. Manage them in the **admin panel**: click **Admin panel** at the top of the Studio, then **Authors**.

## Adding an author

Under **Add author**, fill in:

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Email** | What they sign in with. |
| **Full name** | Optional. If you leave it blank, they're asked for it when they first sign in. |
| **Role** | **Viewer**, **editor**, or **admin**. Students get **editor**. See [Who can do what](/studio/help/who-can-do-what). |
| **Temporary password** | At least 12 characters, typed twice. |

Click **Create author**, and give them the temporary password **in person or over a trusted channel**, not in a shared document. They must choose their own the first time they sign in.

> **Example:** For a new student: their UMass email, role **editor**, and a temporary password like `lilac-meadow-41-rain`. Tell them to go to the Studio and sign in.

## Changing an author

Click **Edit** next to them in the list.

- **Email, full name, role**: change and click **Save changes**. Changing their email signs them out.
- **Reset password**: type a new temporary password twice and click **Reset password**. It signs them out everywhere, and they must choose their own when they next sign in. Use this when someone forgets their password.
- **Suspend**: signs them out and stops them signing in. **Everything they made stays**, and you can **Reactivate** them later. Use this when a student leaves the project.

## Deleting an author

Deleting removes the account for good. **Suspending is usually the better choice.**

If they own artifacts or trails, you choose an active editor or admin to **give them to**, so nothing is lost. Their name stays in each artifact's history as it was. To confirm, type their email exactly, then click **Delete this author**.

## Rules that protect you

- You can't change your own role, reset your own password here (use your profile), or delete yourself. Ask another admin.
- No change can leave the Studio with **no active admin**.
- Two authors can't share an email address.
