## Your account

Your instructor creates your Studio account and gives you two things: the **email address** it uses and a **temporary password**. There's no sign-up page; if you don't have an account, ask your instructor.

## Signing in the first time

1. Go to **https://pintrail.cs.umass.edu/studio** and sign in with your email and the temporary password.
2. The Studio asks you to **choose your own password**. Type the temporary password as your *current password*, then your new one twice.
3. Your new password must be **at least 12 characters**. A short sentence you'll remember works well.

> **Tip:** A phrase like `green roofs hold rain` is long, easy to remember, and much harder to guess than `Pintrail1!`.

You can't do anything else until you've chosen a password. That way a password your instructor knows never stays in use.

## Your profile

Next the Studio asks for your profile. Only your **full name** is required.

| Field | What it's for | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Full name** | How the Studio names you everywhere. | *Paige Turner* |
| **Display name** | What people call you, if it's different. Shown instead of your full name. | *Paige* |
| **Pronouns** | Optional. | *she/her* |
| **Affiliation** | Your class or role. | *COMPSCI 326 student* |
| **Short bio** | A sentence or two. Optional. | *Interested in campus energy systems.* |
| **Photo** | Helps classmates and reviewers know who's who. | A clear photo of your face |

Your name and photo appear on the artifacts you add, in the review list, and in each artifact's history, so people see who wrote what instead of an email address.

**Adding a photo:** click **Add a photo** and pick an image. On a phone you can take one with the camera. The Studio crops it to a square from the middle, turns it the right way up, and removes location information stored in the photo.

## Changing your profile or password later

Click your **name or photo** at the top right of the Studio. Your profile page has a link to change your password too.

## Signing out

Click **Sign out** at the top right. Do this on a shared or public computer.

> **Note:** Forgot your password? For now, ask your instructor (or an admin) to reset it. They'll give you a new temporary password, and you'll choose your own again when you sign in.
