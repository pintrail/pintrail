This page is for **admins**. Only admins can approve artifacts and topics, or send them back.

## The review list

Click **Review** in the sidebar. The number next to it is how many are waiting. The list shows:

- **Waiting for review**: submitted, oldest first, with who submitted it and when.
- **Sent back, not resubmitted yet**: what you've returned, with your note.
- **No owner**: artifacts added before authorship was recorded. Open one and use **Edit** to give it to an author, so they can work on it.

Click an artifact to open it.

## Approving

Open the artifact, check it, and click **Approve** in its **Review** card. It appears in the app, as long as everything it's inside is approved too.

What to check:

- the **pin** is on the right spot (zoom in);
- the **facts** match the **source links**;
- the **description** is in the author's own words and readable on a phone;
- the **photos** are theirs to use, with no faces without permission;
- the **kind** and **tags** are sensible and reuse existing tags.

## Sending back

Open **Send back for changes** in the **Review** card, write what needs to change, and click **Send back**. The artifact becomes a Draft again, and the author sees your note at the top of its Review card.

> **Example:** *"Good start. The pin is on the building next door; move it onto the green roof. And the 40% figure needs a source: add it as a link."*

Be specific: say what to change and, if you can, how. You can also send back something already approved.

## Your own changes

When an admin changes an approved artifact, it stays approved. When anyone else does, it goes back to **Ready for review**.

## Artifacts with no owner

An artifact with no owner can be submitted, withdrawn, and changed only by an admin. Give it an owner with **Edit** → **Owner**. See [Owners and recovering deleted work](/studio/help/owners-and-recovery#giving-an-artifact-to-someone-else).
