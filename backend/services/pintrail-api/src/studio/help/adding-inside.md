Many things worth knowing belong to a building: its roof, its heating system, a sign in its lobby. Add those **inside** the building, so the app can show them together. See [Artifacts inside artifacts](/studio/help/nesting) for why.

## How

1. Open the building (or whatever the new artifact belongs to).
2. Scroll down and click **+ Add one inside** (or **+ Add a nested artifact** if nothing's inside it yet).
3. The New artifact form opens with **Inside (parent)** already set.
4. Give it a name. **Leave the location as it is**, unless it really stands somewhere else.
5. Click **Create**, then fill it in like any other artifact.

You can add an artifact inside **anyone's** artifact, not just your own. The new one is yours.

> **Example:** Open *Integrative Learning Center*, click **+ Add one inside**, name it *Green roof*, leave the location, and create it. It appears under the building in the list, and its page says its location is *inherited from Integrative Learning Center*.

## The location

The map opens on the parent's location, shown as a **hollow, dashed pin**, and the coordinate boxes show the parent's numbers in grey. That means *use the parent's location*, which is right for anything in or on the building.

If it's really somewhere else, like a rain garden across the plaza:

- **drag the pin** to the right spot, or click the map. The pin turns solid: the artifact now has its own location.
- **Clear (use parent's)** puts it back.

See [Location](/studio/help/location#using-the-parent-s-location).

## Choosing the parent on the form

You can also click **+ New** and choose the parent from **Inside (parent)**. The list shows every artifact; the map jumps to the one you pick.

## Put it in the wrong place?

**Edit** the artifact and choose a different **Inside (parent)**. Everything nested inside it moves with it. If it was using its old parent's location, it now uses the new parent's.
