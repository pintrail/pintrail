Adding an artifact takes two steps. First you **create** it with just its name and where it is. Then you **fill it in** on its own page.

## Step 1: create it

Click **+ New** at the top of the sidebar. The form asks for three things:

| Field | What to enter | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Name** | What people call it. See [naming an artifact](/studio/help/artifacts#naming-an-artifact). | *Lot 49 solar canopy* |
| **Inside (parent)** | Leave it as **none (top level)** for a building or anything standing on its own. For something in or on another artifact, choose that artifact. | *none* |
| **Location** | Click the map on the exact spot. **Required for a top-level artifact.** See [Location](/studio/help/location). | A pin on the canopy |

Click **Create**. The artifact opens, and appears in the list on the left.

> **Tip:** Adding something that belongs to a building, like its roof? Open the building and use **+ Add one inside** instead: the parent is filled in for you, and it uses the building's location. See [Adding an artifact inside another](/studio/help/adding-inside).

If something's missing, the form stays open with a red note at the top saying what to fix, and keeps everything you typed. The most common one is a top-level artifact with no location: place it on the map, or choose the artifact it sits in.

## Step 2: fill it in

Your new artifact's page shows a checklist, **Created. Next, fill it in**:

1. **A description, a kind, and tags.** Click **Edit** (in the checklist or at the top of the page).
   - The [kind](/studio/help/kinds) starts as `other`; choose the right one.
   - Write the [description](/studio/help/writing-descriptions): what it is, why it matters, how it works.
   - Add a few [tags](/studio/help/tags), reusing ones that already exist.
2. **At least one source link**, in the **Links** card on the page. See [Source links](/studio/help/links).
3. **A photo you took**, in the **Media** card. See [Photos and PDFs](/studio/help/media).
4. **Then submit it for review**, in the **Review** card. See [Submitting for review](/studio/help/submitting-for-review).

Each item is ticked off as you do it. While a draft is still missing something, its page keeps the checklist, as **Finish this artifact**, so you can see at a glance what's left.

> **Example:** You create *Lot 49 solar canopy* with a pin on the canopy. Then: **Edit**, kind `installation`, a description that says what the canopy is, how much power it makes (from your source), and why it's there, tags *solar* and *energy*, save. Add the page your figures came from as a link, upload a photo you took of the canopy, and submit it for review.

## Linking it to a topic

If the artifact belongs to a shared story, like a building that's LEED certified, link it to that [topic](/studio/help/topics) in its **Topics** card. When you start from a topic's page with **+ Add a new artifact linked here**, the link is made for you. See [Linking artifacts to a topic](/studio/help/linking-topics).

## Why two steps?

So you can add an artifact quickly, standing in front of it, with just a name and your location, and fill in the rest later at a desk.
