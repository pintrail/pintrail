Every artifact needs a place, because that's how the Pintrail app knows someone is near it. The place is a **pin on the map**, stored as two numbers: **latitude** and **longitude**.

> **Example:** The Integrative Learning Center is at latitude `42.3910`, longitude `-72.5259`. Copy a pair like `42.3910, -72.5259` from Google Maps and paste it into the latitude box, and the Studio splits it for you.

## Placing the pin

On the New artifact or Edit form:

1. **Zoom in** with the **+** button or by scrolling, until you can see the building or spot clearly.
2. **Click the map** where the artifact is. A pin appears and the latitude and longitude boxes fill in.
3. **Drag the pin** to adjust it, or click somewhere else.

You can also type or paste numbers into the boxes; the pin moves to match. On a phone, **Use my location** puts the pin where you're standing. See [Using the Studio on your phone](/studio/help/phone).

## Check the pin

The pin is what the app uses to tell someone they've arrived, so put it **on the thing itself**: the right building, not the one next door, and the entrance side if that's where people approach.

> **Watch out:** Zoomed out, a pin can look right and be a whole building off. Zoom all the way in before you save.

## Using the parent's location

A **top-level** artifact (one with no parent) must have its own pin. A **nested** artifact doesn't have to: it can use its parent's.

When you add something inside a building, the map shows the building's location as a **hollow, dashed pin**, and the coordinate boxes show the building's numbers in grey. Leave it like that for anything in or on the building: a roof, a room, a system inside. After you save, the artifact's page says the location is **inherited from** the building.

Only give a nested artifact its own pin if it really stands somewhere else, like a rain garden across the plaza. Drag the pin or click the map, and it becomes solid. **Clear (use parent's)** puts it back.

> **Tip:** Using the parent's location is better than copying the same numbers. If someone later moves the building's pin to the right spot, everything inside moves with it.

## How accurate is it?

Map clicks are as accurate as you zoom. **Use my location** on a phone is usually within 5 to 15 metres outdoors, and much worse indoors, so always check the pin afterwards and drag it if needed.

## Topics have no location

A [topic](/studio/help/topics) is an idea, not a place, so it has no pin. The artifacts linked to it each have their own.
